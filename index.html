Ferdinand Magellan

Sailed for
Both Spain and Portugal (his famous voyage was for Spain)
Explored
Around South America, across the Pacific to the Philippines
When
1505–1521
  • The first to sail past South America. He found the Strait of Magellan.
  • His expedition was the first to circumnavigate the globe (sail all the way around it).
  • Killed in the Philippines in 1521, but his crew made it home in 1522.

Heads-up: the guide says the crew made it back to Portugal. Their ship, the Victoria, actually landed in Spain.

RememberHis expedition went around the world. Magellan himself did not make it home.

What is the difference?

Columbian Exchange vs. Triangular Trade

2 vs 3

The Columbian Exchange went two ways, between Europe and the Americas. The Triangular Trade had three legs, and only the Triangular Trade carried enslaved people.

Columbian Exchange

A two-way exchange of goods and traditions between Europe and the Americas.

  • Europe → AmericasCattle, horses, wheat, rye, smallpox
  • Americas → EuropeTobacco, potatoes, squash, cocoa, syphilis

Triangular Trade

A three-way exchange of goods, traditions, and human cargo: from the Americas to Europe to Africa and back to the Americas.

  • Americas → EuropeRaw materials: cotton, tobacco, lumber, and more
  • Europe → AfricaIron, guns, liquor
  • Africa → AmericasEnslaved Africans. This leg is called the Middle Passage, and it was the slave trade.

Questions from the guide

Culture in the Americas

What culture was created in the Americas as a result of exploration? How did Christianity play a role?

Encomienda
Like a plantation, but it used the local Native population as its source of labor. The guide says workers were paid for their work.

In practice, many Native workers were forced to work and were treated very harshly.

Missionaries
Christian missionaries tried to convert the local population to Christianity.

What were some of the consequences of the plantation system in the Americas?

Slavery
Plantations needed huge numbers of workers. As Native people died from disease (like smallpox) and overwork, plantation owners brought enslaved Africans across the Middle Passage.
